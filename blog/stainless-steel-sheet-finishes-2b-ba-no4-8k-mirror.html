Stainless Steel Sheet Finishes: 2B, BA, No.4 and 8K Mirror Explained

T
Tatvam Overseas Inc Technical Team Published: 2026-10-21  |  Last updated: 2026-10-21
Direct Answer

Quick answer: Stainless steel sheet finishes range from rough hot-rolled No.1 to mirror-like 8K. 2B is the smooth, general-purpose cold-rolled finish. BA is brighter and more reflective. No.4 is a brushed satin finish that hides scratches, and 8K is a polished mirror finish. Pick the finish by look, cleaning needs and how the sheet will be formed or fabricated.

Key Technical Takeaways

  • ✓ Mill finishes (No.1, 2D, 2B, BA) are created during production at the steel rolling mill without post-processing abrasive polishing.
  • ✓ 2B is the global standard baseline finish for fabrication, deep drawing, and subsequent mechanical polishing operations.
  • ✓ Bright Annealed (BA) provides high mill-direct reflectivity without buffing lines, ideal for appliance trims and reflectors.
  • ✓ No.4 brushed satin and Hairline (HL) finishes diffuse light and conceal surface fingerprints, making them standard for elevators and kitchen equipment.
  • ✓ 8K / No.8 mirror offers true specular reflection; it requires strict protective film handling during fabrication and installation.

What Are the Main Stainless Steel Sheet Finishes?

Stainless steel surface finishes are standardized globally to ensure consistency in reflectivity, roughness (Ra values), and mechanical fabrication performance. The table below outlines the primary finishes recognized under international manufacturing standards:

Finish How it is made Look Typical uses
No.1 (HR) Hot rolled, annealed and pickled Dull, rough, matte grey Thick plates, tanks, industrial equipment, heat-resistant parts
2D Cold rolled, annealed and pickled Dull, non-reflective, even Deep-drawn parts, components that will be coated or finished later
2B Cold rolled, annealed, pickled and lightly skin-passed Smooth, moderately reflective The most common general-purpose finish; also the base for polishing
BA Cold rolled and bright annealed in a protective atmosphere Highly reflective, bright Appliances, trims, reflectors, architectural panels
No.4 Mechanically polished to a directional grain Brushed satin Kitchen equipment, food service, lift interiors, wall panels
Hairline (HL) Polished with a long continuous line pattern Fine linear brushed look Architectural cladding and decorative panels
No.8 / 8K Polished with progressively finer abrasives, then buffed Mirror, very reflective Decorative panels, signage, lift cabins, high-end interiors

Finish names follow ASTM A480 and EN 10088-2 conventions. “8K” is a trade term for the mirror finish, so confirm what a supplier means by it when specifying decorative cladding.

How Are Finishes Created?

Mill finishes (No.1, 2D, 2B, BA) are produced during rolling, annealing and pickling at the steel mill. Polished and decorative finishes (No.4, hairline, 8K) are made afterwards by mechanical polishing, usually starting from a 2B sheet. Coloured Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) and etched finishes are applied after that, creating specialty architectural surfaces like those available in our Decorative Stainless Steel Sheets Portfolio.

2B vs. BA: What Is the Difference?

Both are cold-rolled and smooth, but BA is annealed in a protective atmosphere (typically hydrogen or nitrogen gas), which avoids the oxide scale layer that has to be acid-pickled off 2B. The result is a brighter, more reflective surface. It is not as perfect as a mechanically polished 8K mirror. Choose 2B when you need an economical, versatile finish for fabrication or stamping, and BA when reflectivity matters but full mirror polishing isn’t needed.

When Should You Choose No.4, Hairline or 8K?

No.4 (Brushed Satin):

Hides fingerprints and minor scratches, and is widely used where equipment gets handled and cleaned often, such as commercial kitchens, beverage counters, and elevators.

Hairline (HL):

Features a finer, more continuous linear grain pattern produced with specialized grit belts that creates a subtle, elegant texture for premium architectural facades and lobby walls.

8K Mirror:

The most reflective choice available in metal panels. Because it displays reflections with optical clarity, it shows fingerprints, smudges, and any substrate distortion, requiring careful handling and maintenance.

How Do You Choose the Right Finish?

  • Environment: For coastal or humid locations, the base metal grade matters more than the finish. Specify SS 304 Sheets for inland architecture, or SS 316L Marine Plates within 5 km of coastlines. A smoother surface (like BA or electropolished) also helps shed airborne salts and resist staining. If considering lower-cost grades for interior use, review our SS 202 vs SS 304 Comparison.
  • Appearance: Decide whether your design aesthetic requires matte (2B), directional satin (No.4 / Hairline), or specular reflection (8K Mirror).
  • Handling and Cleaning: Brushed finishes hide everyday wear and contact marks significantly better than high-gloss mirror panels.
  • Fabrication: Deep drawing, press braking, and roll forming usually start from 2B or 2D sheets. High-end polishing is typically performed after forming where complex geometries are involved.
  • Protective Film: Keep protective laser-compatible PE film intact throughout cutting, bending, and transport, removing it only upon final site installation.

Practical Engineering Tips:

Always match the grain direction across all adjacent panels in a project to prevent optical shading mismatches. Furthermore, communicate the target grade, thickness, and finish to your supplier upfront, because not every finish is standard across heavy-gauge plate stock.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most common stainless steel sheet finish? ↓
2B is the most widely used finish. It is smooth, economical and a common base for further polishing.
Is BA finish the same as 8K mirror? ↓
No. BA is bright annealed at the mill and highly reflective. 8K is a polished mirror finish applied afterwards, and it is more reflective.
Which finish is best for kitchens and food equipment? ↓
A No.4 brushed finish is a popular choice because it hides fingerprints and scratches and is easy to clean. The grade matters too: use 304 or 316L for food-contact and wet areas.
Can a 2B sheet be polished later? ↓
Yes. 2B is a common starting point for No.4, hairline and mirror finishes. The result depends on the abrasives and the processing used.
