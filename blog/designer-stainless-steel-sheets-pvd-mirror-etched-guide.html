Designer Stainless Steel Sheets: PVD Colours, Mirror, Hairline and Etched Finishes

Key Takeaways

  • ✓ Designer sheets are standard SS 304 or 202 sheets with a decorative surface: PVD colour, 8K mirror, hairline, etched or embossed.
  • ✓ SS 304 is the preferred base grade for most decorative applications. Use SS 202 only for dry indoor work.
  • ✓ Order the whole job from one batch to ensure consistent colour and finish across panels.
  • ✓ Always request sample pieces to approve colour and finish before bulk supply.

Designer stainless steel sheets are used wherever steel needs to look as good as it performs: lift cabins, wall cladding, reception desks, ceilings, furniture and signage. But choosing between PVD colours, mirror, hairline and etched patterns can be confusing. This guide explains each option and what to check before you order.

What Counts as a Designer or Decorative Sheet?

A designer sheet is a stainless steel sheet whose surface has been given a special finish or pattern for appearance. The base is usually SS 304 or SS 202 sheet, and the decoration is added by polishing, coating, etching or embossing.

Common Designer Finishes

PVD Colour Coating

Physical vapour deposition applies a very thin coloured layer in shades such as gold, rose gold, black, bronze, blue and champagne. It is popular for lift doors, wall panels and luxury interiors. The colour is applied over a base finish, often mirror or hairline.

Mirror (8K)

A highly polished, reflective surface. It looks striking but shows fingerprints and scratches easily, so it suits low-touch areas or panels that can be cleaned often.

Hairline and No.4 (Brushed)

A fine linear grain that hides minor scratches and fingerprints. It is the most practical choice for high-traffic surfaces. See our guide on sheet finishes: 2B, BA, No.4 and 8K for how these base finishes are made.

Etched Patterns

A chemical process creates patterns or textures on the surface, sometimes combined with colour. It is used for feature walls, lift interiors and decorative panels.

Embossed and Textured Sheets

Raised patterns are pressed into the sheet. They add grip and depth and also hide wear.

Which Grade Should You Choose?

Grade Best For Notes
SS 304Interiors, lifts, kitchens, any area with moistureStandard choice for most decorative applications
SS 316LCoastal locations, chemical exposureBetter chloride resistance than 304
SS 202Dry, indoor decorative workLower cost but less corrosion resistance than 304

Tips Before You Order

  1. Order the whole job from one batch. Colour and finish can vary slightly between batches, so panels that must match should come from the same lot.
  2. Check grain direction. For brushed and hairline sheets, keep the grain running the same way across all panels during fabrication.
  3. Keep the protective film on until installation is finished, to avoid scratches.
  4. Ask for sample pieces to approve the colour and finish before bulk supply.
  5. Clean gently. Use mild soap and a soft cloth. Avoid abrasive pads and harsh chemicals, especially on PVD and mirror finishes.
  6. Confirm the specification in writing: grade, thickness, size, finish, colour and quantity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PVD coating durable? ↓
PVD is hard and generally resistant to fading, but it can be damaged by abrasive cleaning or sharp impact. Handle and clean as recommended.
