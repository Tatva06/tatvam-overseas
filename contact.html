Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries about stock availability, shipping logistics, and material certification.

Do you provide Mill Test Certificates (MTC)?

Yes. Our supplies are accompanied by Mill Test Certificates (MTC) per EN 10204 3.1, certifying chemical composition and mechanical properties, with heat number traceability from the mill.

Do you export materials internationally?

Yes! We export to over 15 countries including UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kenya, Nigeria, and Southeast Asia. We handle all logistics including sea-worthy wooden pallet packing, container stuffing, and complete documentation (Commercial Invoice, Packing List, COA, Fumigation Certificate, Certificate of Origin).

What payment terms do you accept?

We work on advance payment. For domestic orders, payment is accepted by RTGS/NEFT. For exports, payment is by bank transfer (TT). Payment terms are confirmed in your quotation.

Can you cut material to custom sizes?

Cutting, beveling and processing are arranged through our processing partners and are usually completed in about 2-3 working days. Charges apply based on complexity and quantity.
