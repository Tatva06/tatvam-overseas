What Are Stainless Steel 304 Sheets & Plates and Where Are They Used?

Tatvam Overseas Inc is a stockist, supplier, and exporter of Stainless Steel 304 and 304L sheets, plates, shim sheets, and slit coils based in Mumbai, India. Operating from Mumbai, we supply standard and customized thicknesses ranging from 0.5 mm up to 50 mm as part of our Stainless Steel Product Portfolio.

Grade 304 (UNS S30400 / 1.4301) is an austenitic chromium-nickel stainless steel, renowned for exceptional weldability, deep-drawing capability, and resistance to atmospheric oxidation and mild chemical media. Grade 304L (UNS S30403 / 1.4307) features an ultra-low carbon chemistry (C ≤ 0.030%) that eliminates harmful chromium carbide precipitation during welding, making it the preferred specification for heavy-gauge architectural, pharmaceutical, and chemical process equipment without requiring post-weld annealing.

For demanding marine or coastal applications where chloride pitting is a risk, engineers frequently compare SS 304 against molybdenum-bearing SS 316L. Learn more in our technical guide: SS 304 vs SS 316L Comparison Guide.

What Factors Determine SS 304 Sheet & Plate Prices in Mumbai?

Stainless steel 304 pricing is dynamic and reflects several key commercial and technical determinants:

  • LME Nickel & Chromium Indices: Base metal alloy surcharges fluctuate with monthly global raw material prices.
  • Thickness & Rolling Process: Thin cold-rolled gauges (0.5 mm - 1.2 mm) carry higher cold-reduction rolling charges than thick hot-rolled plates.
  • Surface Finish: No.4 brushed hairline with laser PVC film, 8K mirror polishing, or protective coatings involve additional processing premiums over standard 2B mill finish.
  • Volume & Custom Cutting: Cutting arranged on request through our processing partners; charges as per quotation.
