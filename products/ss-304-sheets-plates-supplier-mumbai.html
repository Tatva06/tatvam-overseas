Material Overview & Specifications

Tatvam Overseas Inc is a leading stockist, supplier, and exporter of prime-quality Stainless Steel 304 and 304L sheets, plates, shim sheets, and slit coils based in Mumbai, India. Operating from our central Girgaon sales office and Kalbadevi stock facility, we maintain over 250 metric tons of ready inventory across standard and customized thicknesses ranging from 0.5 mm up to 50 mm.

Grade 304 (UNS S30400 / 1.4301) is the quintessential austenitic chromium-nickel stainless steel, renowned for exceptional weldability, deep-drawing capability, and resistance to atmospheric oxidation and mild chemical media. Grade 304L (UNS S30403 / 1.4307) features an ultra-low carbon chemistry (C ≤ 0.030%) that eliminates harmful chromium carbide precipitation during welding, making it the preferred specification for heavy-gauge architectural, pharmaceutical, and chemical process equipment without requiring post-weld annealing.

All our stainless steel 304 sheets and plates conform rigorously to ASTM A240 / ASME SA240 standards. We offer versatile surface finishes including Cold Rolled 2B, No.4 Hairline brush with PVC laser protection film, Scotch-Brite, BA (Bright Annealed), and Hot Rolled No.1 pickled finish for heavy structural applications. Our in-house computerized shear and high-precision fiber laser cutting machinery allow us to provide cut-to-size blanks, strips, and circles within a tight dimensional tolerance of ±0.5 mm.

Whether your project requires single prototype sheets or multi-container export consignments, Tatvam Overseas Inc delivers complete material traceability. Every single shipment is backed by an authentic Mill Test Certificate conforming to EN 10204 Type 3.1, verifying exact heat numbers, optical emission spectrometry chemical analysis, and mechanical tensile tests.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between SS 304 and SS 304L plates? ↓
The primary distinction lies in carbon content: SS 304 contains up to 0.08% carbon, whereas SS 304L has a maximum of 0.03% carbon. The lower carbon in 304L prevents intergranular carbide precipitation during welding, preserving corrosion resistance in as-welded assemblies.
