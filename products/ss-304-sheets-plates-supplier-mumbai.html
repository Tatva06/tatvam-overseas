SS 304 Sheets & Plates: Overview, Metallurgy & Applications

Tatvam Overseas Inc operates as an independent stockist and dealer of Stainless Steel 304 and 304L sheets, plates, shim coils, and slit strips from Mumbai's central steel marketplace. Sourced from leading domestic mills including Jindal Stainless as well as certified international producers, our inventory spans cold-rolled thin gauges from 0.5 mm up to heavy hot-rolled industrial plates of 50 mm as part of our Stainless Steel Product Range.

Grade 304 (UNS S30400 / EN 1.4301) is the quintessential 18/8 austenitic stainless steel, alloying 18% chromium with 8% nickel to deliver exceptional deep-drawing capability, ductile weldability, and resistance to atmospheric oxidation. Grade 304L (UNS S30403 / EN 1.4307) provides an ultra-low carbon chemistry (C ≤ 0.030%) preventing intergranular chromium carbide precipitation during welding, making it the premier choice for pressure vessels, brewery tanks, and pharmaceutical fabrication without subsequent post-weld annealing.

When engineering for marine or severe coastal exposures where chlorides provoke pitting, compare SS 304 with molybdenum-alloyed SS 316L in our guide: SS 304 vs SS 316L Comparison Guide.

If you are evaluating cost-sensitive dry interior applications, review our technical breakdown: SS 202 vs SS 304 Differences & Buying Guide. What is the difference between SS 304 and SS 202 sheets? ↓
SS 304 contains 18-20% chromium and 8-10.5% nickel, providing superior atmospheric and chemical corrosion resistance for food equipment, vessels, and outdoor use. SS 202 replaces most nickel with manganese and nitrogen (1-2% nickel), making it more economical for indoor decorative panels, utensils, and dry furniture. Read our detailed SS 202 vs SS 304 Buying Guide.
Is SS 304 stainless steel rust-proof? ↓
SS 304 forms a self-healing chromium oxide passive film that prevents rust in normal atmospheric, freshwater, and mild chemical environments. However, in aggressive coastal marine air or high-chloride environments, SS 316L (with 2-3% molybdenum) is recommended to prevent pitting corrosion. See our SS 304 vs SS 316L Comparison.
Which surface finish is best for kitchens, elevators, or architectural cladding? ↓
For commercial kitchens and food equipment, 2B (mill finish) and No.4 hairline (brushed satin) are industry standards due to easy sanitization. For elevator cabins and architectural cladding, No.4 hairline, 8K mirror, and PVD titanium color coated sheets are preferred for aesthetics and scratch concealment.
