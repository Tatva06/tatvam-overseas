Frequently Asked Questions About Stainless Steel Supply

What factors determine stainless steel prices in Mumbai? ↓
Stainless steel prices in Mumbai depend primarily on global London Metal Exchange (LME) nickel and molybdenum prices, raw material alloy surcharges, sheet thickness, surface finish (2B, No.4, Mirror, or PVD), order volume, and domestic currency exchange rates.
What sizes, thicknesses, and finishes are available in ready stock? ↓
We stock thicknesses from 0.5 mm to 50 mm for sheets and plates in widths of 1000 mm, 1250 mm, 1500 mm, and 2000 mm. Seamless and ERW pipes range from 1/4 inch to 24 inch NB. Finishes include 2B, BA, No.4 Hairline with laser film, Mirror 8K, and PVD titanium colors.
What manufacturing standards and MTC documentation do you supply? ↓
All stainless steel products conform to ASTM A240, ASTM A312, ASTM A276, and ASME dimensional codes. Every shipment is accompanied by an EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificate conforming to EN 10204 Type 3.1 with heat number traceability; PMI testing on request.
What payment terms do you offer for domestic and export buyers? ↓
We work on advance payment. For domestic orders, payment is accepted by RTGS/NEFT. For exports, payment is by bank transfer (TT). Payment terms are confirmed in your quotation.
