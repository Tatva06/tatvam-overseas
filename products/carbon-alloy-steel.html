Frequently Asked Questions About Carbon & Alloy Steel Pipes

What factors determine carbon and alloy steel pipe pricing in Mumbai? ↓
Alloy and carbon steel pipe pricing is governed by chromium and molybdenum raw material surcharges, whether the pipe is seamless or ERW, heavy-wall schedule requirements (Sch 80, Sch 160, XXS), Indian Boiler Regulations (IBR) inspection and Form III-C stamping fees, and total order tonnage.
What sizes, wall thicknesses, and schedules are stocked in Mumbai? ↓
We maintain ready inventory of seamless alloy and carbon steel pipes from 1/2 inch NB up to 24 inch NB in Schedules 40, 80, 160, and XXS. Heavy-wall boiler tubing in custom wall thicknesses from 3.0 mm up to 45.0 mm is also available.
What standards and IBR certifications accompany these pipes? ↓
All boiler pipes strictly comply with ASTM A335 (P11, P22, P91), ASTM A106 Gr. B, and API 5L Grade B. Consignments are certified under Indian Boiler Regulations (IBR) with IBR Form III-A certificates stamped by an authorized Boiler Inspector.
What payment terms and mill origins do you offer? ↓
We work on advance payment. For domestic orders, payment is accepted by RTGS/NEFT. For exports, payment is by bank transfer (TT). Payment terms are confirmed in your quotation.
