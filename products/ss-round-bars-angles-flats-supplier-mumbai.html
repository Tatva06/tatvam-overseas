What Factors Determine Stainless Steel Bar and Profile Prices in Mumbai?

Stainless steel bar and profile prices vary based on finishing and tolerance:

  • Cold Drawn vs Centerless Ground: Precision tolerance ground bars (h8, h9) carry machining fees over rough black forged bars.
  • Material Grade: SS 316L bars with 2-3% Mo carry a 25% to 35% alloy premium over standard 304.
  • Custom Band Saw Cutting: High-precision cut blanks and billets are supplied with zero end wastage.
  • Order Weight & Quantity: Bundled tonnage consignments receive bulk wholesale pricing.
