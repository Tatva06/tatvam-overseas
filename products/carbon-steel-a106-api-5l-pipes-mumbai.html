ASTM A106 Grade B is the benchmark specification for seamless carbon steel pipe engineered for high-temperature and high-pressure service in power stations, oil refining units, and industrial steam boilers. API 5L Grade B (PSL-1 & PSL-2) governs steel pipe suitable for the transportation of oil, gas, and hydrocarbon slurries in pipeline transportation systems. Our pipes feature dual certification (ASTM A106 Gr B / API 5L Gr B) to afford maximum versatility across structural and process piping designs.

What Factors Determine ASTM A106 Carbon Steel Pipe Prices in Mumbai?

Carbon steel pipe pricing is governed by base steel benchmarks and manufacturing specs:

  • Domestic & Global Steel Indices: Domestic primary mill base prices (JSL, MSL, Jindal Saw) adjusted monthly.
  • Wall Schedule (Sch 40 vs Sch 80/160): Higher wall schedules contain significantly more steel per meter, directly impacting cost.
  • IBR Stamping vs Non-IBR: Pipes certified under Indian Boiler Regulations carry statutory certification and inspection fees.
  • Protective Coating: 3LPE coating or custom external epoxy treatments involve specialized application costs.
