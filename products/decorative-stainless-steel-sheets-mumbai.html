Architectural Stainless Steel: Finishes, Technology & Applications

Modern architectural design increasingly demands reflective depth, dynamic textures, and corrosion-resistant elegance. Decorative Stainless Steel Finishes Stocked

1. PVD Titanium Color Sheets

Colors: Titanium Gold, Rose Gold, Black Titanium, Champagne Gold, Bronze

Physical Vapor Deposition creates an atomic-scale vapor bond of titanium nitride onto mirror or hairline stainless steel. Highly scratch-resistant, hypoallergenic, and non-tarnishing in indoor applications. Widely specified for hotel atrium columns, feature wall panels, and bespoke metal furniture.

2. 8K Super Mirror Finish

Distortion-free optical clarity (available in raw silver and PVD colored)

Achieved through progressive multi-stage rotary felt buffing with cerium oxide slurry, completely eliminating surface waviness and orange peel. Delivers optical reflection comparable to fine silvered float glass with the unbreakable safety of high-tensile stainless steel.

3. Hairline (Brushed Satin / No.4) Finish

Continuous unidirectional fine abrasive grain

A classic linear satin finish created by uniform abrasive grit belts. Offers a soft non-glare metallic luster that actively conceals handling marks and minor scratches. The standard choice for commercial elevator landing doors, reception counter claddings, and kitchen backsplashes.

4. Chemical Acid-Etched Textures

Custom geometric, floral, and elevator cabin designs

Chemical acid-etching applies silk-screened photo-resist masks before chemical milling to etch intricate relief patterns into mirror or hairline sheets. Available in single-tone matte/mirror contrast or two-tone PVD colored relief for luxury elevator cabin interiors.

5. Embossed & 3D Water Ripple Sheets

Fluid liquid-metal ripples, linen, diamond, and barfi patterns

Hydraulically pressed or roll-embossed to produce three-dimensional tactile surfaces. 3D Water Ripple sheets (small, medium, large waves in mirror or PVD colors) scatter ambient light organically and are the leading trend for luxury hotel ceilings, nightclub bars, and statement entryways.

Designer Stainless Steel Sheet Price: What Influences Project Costs?

Designer stainless steel sheets are quoted on a per-sheet or per-square-meter basis. Pricing reflects multiple engineering factors:

1. Base Alloy Selection (304 vs 202) SS 304 base sheets carry a premium over SS 202 but are mandatory for exterior cladding, humid restrooms, and coastal projects.
2. Surface Treatment Multipliers Multi-stage 8K mirror polishing, vacuum PVD color chambers, chemical etching stencils, and hydraulic ripple stamping add specific processing costs.
3. Sheet Thickness Gauge Heavier gauges (1.2 mm to 2.0 mm) provide structural rigidity for large elevator panels without oil-canning, affecting sheet weight and cost.
4. Protective Coatings & Quantity Double-layer laser film and Anti-Fingerprint (AFP) nano-sealing are factored into project quotes. Volume orders benefit from tier discounts.

Can decorative PVD stainless steel sheets be laser cut and bent without cracking? ↓
Yes. Our decorative sheets are protected with double-layer laser PE film (70-100 microns) designed for fiber laser cutting with clean nitrogen assist. The PVD coating is deposited at the molecular level and bends up to 90 degrees on CNC press brakes without chipping, flaking, or crazing.
Does PVD colour fade, peel, or tarnish over time? ↓
Physical Vapor Deposition (PVD) titanium nitride coating forms an atomic bond with the stainless steel substrate. Unlike electroplating or paint, it does not peel or blister. For interior architectural installations, PVD colors retain their vibrant luster for decades when maintained with neutral cleaners.
Which grade should I choose: SS 304 or SS 202 for decorative panels? ↓
Choose SS 304 for exterior facades, coastal buildings, bathrooms, wet kitchens, and high-spec public infrastructure. Choose SS 202 for dry interior feature walls, hotel reception desks, false ceiling panels, and luxury furniture trims where cost economy is desired. Read our SS 202 vs 304 Guide.
What is Anti-Fingerprint (AFP) nano-coating and is it available? ↓
Anti-Fingerprint (AFP) is a transparent hydrophobic polymer nano-layer applied over PVD or hairline surfaces. It dramatically reduces finger smudges, resists oil and water stains, and simplifies cleaning in high-touch elevator cabins, doors, and reception counters.
