Material Overview & Capabilities

Tatvam Overseas Inc is a prominent stockist and distributor of premium decorative stainless steel sheets based in Mumbai, India. We supply luxury architectural fabricators, elevator manufacturers, facade contractors, and interior designers across India and international export markets with pristine decorative metal panels.

Our decorative sheets utilize prime cold-rolled austenitic stainless steel (primarily ASTM A240 Grade 304 and 316L for coastal and outdoor durability, with select Grade 201 for cost-effective indoor applications). Through vacuum Cathodic Arc Ion Plating (PVD), titanium and zirconium nitride layers are bonded at the atomic level, delivering radiant lustrous color finishes that are designed for good scratch resistance and colour stability in interior use.

Every decorative sheet is laminated with double-layer laser-compatible PE protective film (70–100 microns) ensuring spotless preservation during CNC laser cutting, V-grooving, press braking, and installation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can decorative PVD stainless steel sheets be laser cut and bent?

Yes. All our decorative sheets ship with specialized laser-protective film (Fiber laser safe) that withstands nitrogen and oxygen CNC laser cutting without edge burring or discoloration. The PVD coating has atomic adhesion, so it bends 90 degrees on press brakes without cracking or flaking.

Does the PVD color fade or tarnish over time?

PVD coatings are designed for good colour stability in interior use. Performance depends on the base material, environment and cleaning. For coastal or exterior use, specify a 316L base and discuss with our team.
