Material Overview & Specifications

Tatvam Overseas Inc is a leading stockist, processor, and exporter of high-integrity Duplex 2205 (UNS S31803 / UNS S32205 / 1.4462) stainless steel plates, seamless pipes, flanges, and round bars based in Mumbai, India. We supply premium duplex alloy products to offshore oil and gas platforms, chemical tankers, desalination plants, and flue-gas desulfurization (FGD) systems worldwide.

Duplex 2205 is a nitrogen-enhanced dual-phase austenitic-ferritic stainless steel characterized by an approximately 50/50 phase balance. This dual microstructure combines the superior chloride stress-corrosion cracking resistance of ferritic steels with the high toughness, weldability, and fabrication ease of austenitic alloys. With approximately 22% Chromium, 5% Nickel, 3% Molybdenum, and 0.17% Nitrogen, Duplex 2205 achieves a Pitting Resistance Equivalent Number (PREN) exceeding 35, delivering nearly double the design yield strength of standard austenitic 316L.

Our Duplex 2205 plates and sheets comply with ASTM A240 / ASME SA240, while our seamless and welded pipes conform to ASTM A790 / A928. Pressure fittings and flanges conform to ASTM A182 Grade F51 / F60. We maintain ready stocks from 1.0 mm sheet up to 60 mm heavy plate, alongside pipe diameters from 1/2 inch NB to 16 inches NB in Schedule 40S and 80S.

Every lot of Duplex 2205 supplied by Tatvam Overseas Inc undergoes microstructural ferrite count determination (ASTM E562), Charpy V-Notch impact testing at -40°C, and ferric chloride pitting corrosion testing in accordance with ASTM G48 Method A. Dispatches include full EN 10204 Type 3.1 Mill Test Certificates with certified heat traceability.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the primary advantages of Duplex 2205 over SS 316L? ↓
Duplex 2205 delivers nearly double the mechanical yield strength of SS 316L, allowing designers to down-gauge wall thicknesses and reduce equipment weight. Furthermore, its PREN 35+ provides vastly superior resistance to chloride pitting and stress corrosion cracking.
What is the significance of the 50/50 phase balance in Duplex 2205? ↓
The balanced ratio of austenite and ferrite phases provides optimal resistance to stress corrosion cracking while maintaining high fracture toughness and impact resistance even at sub-zero temperatures down to -40°C.
Can Duplex 2205 be welded easily? ↓
Yes, Duplex 2205 exhibits good weldability using matching 2209 duplex filler metals. Control of heat input and interpass temperatures (below 150°C) is required to maintain the proper phase balance in the heat-affected zone.
Do you provide ASTM G48 pitting corrosion testing reports? ↓
Yes, our Duplex 2205 test certificates routinely document ASTM G48 Method A ferric chloride pitting testing at 25°C with zero pitting and acceptable weight loss criteria.
