What is SS 202 stainless steel?

SS 202 is an austenitic stainless steel in the 200 series. Manganese and nitrogen replace part of the nickel used in SS 304, which usually makes it more economical, with lower corrosion resistance. It works best in dry indoor use.

Jindal J1 and J4: what is the difference?

J1 and J4 are Jindal 200-series grades with a different balance of alloying elements such as nickel, copper and manganese, and they are positioned for different forming needs. The mill datasheet and the Mill Test Certificate for your lot show the actual composition of the grade supplied.

Where is SS 202 used?

Utensils and kitchenware, furniture and interior trims, dry indoor decorative panels, and general sheet metal fabrication where moisture and chemical exposure are low.

Where should you choose SS 304 instead?

For food and dairy processing, wet kitchens, outdoor or coastal installations, welded tanks and pipework, and anywhere rust staining would be unacceptable, choose SS 304 or SS 316L. Read our guide: SS 202 vs SS 304.

Frequently Asked Questions About SS 202 Sheets

Is SS 202 stainless steel? ↓
Yes. It is an austenitic stainless steel with about 17 to 19% chromium. It has lower corrosion resistance than 304 because part of the nickel is replaced by manganese and nitrogen.
What is the difference between Jindal J1 and J4? ↓
Both are Jindal 200-series grades, balanced differently in alloying elements such as nickel, copper and manganese, and positioned for different forming needs. The mill datasheet and the Mill Test Certificate for your lot show the actual composition.
Which is better for utensils, 202 or 304? ↓
SS 202 is widely used for utensils where cost matters and use is dry or short-contact. SS 304 has better corrosion resistance and is the usual choice for food processing, wet areas and long-term food contact. Check the requirements for your product.
How do I check the grade I receive? ↓
Compare the heat number on the material with the Mill Test Certificate, and ask for PMI on the sheet. A magnet test is not reliable.
