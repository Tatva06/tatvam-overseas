What is SS 202, Jindal J1, and Jindal J4?

Grade 202 is an austenitic chromium-nickel-manganese stainless steel developed as a cost-efficient alternative to Grade 304. By replacing a large fraction of costly nickel with manganese (~7.5–10%) and nitrogen, SS 202 is typically lower cost than SS 304 because of lower nickel content while retaining good formability, high yield strength, and appealing aesthetics for non-aggressive indoor uses.

In India, Jindal Stainless is the premier manufacturer of 200-series alloys, classified into targeted proprietary grades:

  • Jindal J1 Grade: Formulated with copper to soften the matrix, reduce work hardening, and enable deep drawing. This makes J1 the preferred choice for stainless steel cookware, deep utensils, sinks, and severe stamping operations.   • Jindal J4 Grade: Engineered with higher manganese for higher tensile rigidity. J4 is the standard workhorse for general sheet metal fabrication, decorative furniture framing, architectural trims, and elevator door panels. What is the metallurgical difference between Jindal J1 and Jindal J4? ↓
Both are austenitic 200-series grades engineered by Jindal Stainless. Jindal J1 is formulated to provide ductility for deep drawing, kitchen utensils, and complex bending. Jindal J4 provides higher tensile strength for general sheet fabrication, architectural trims, and paneling. Chemical composition is shown on the mill test certificate supplied with each lot.
Is SS 202 stainless steel rust-proof? Can it be used outdoors? ↓
SS 202 offers good corrosion resistance in dry indoor environments and mild atmospheric exposure. However, because manganese replaces a significant portion of nickel, it is not rust-proof in outdoor humid, wet, chemical, or coastal conditions. For outdoor railings or wet kitchens, SS 304 or SS 316L must be specified. Read our full comparison: SS 202 vs SS 304 Guide.
Which is better for kitchenware and fabrication: 202 or 304? ↓
SS 202 is widely favored for domestic utensils, dry racks, and furniture frames where price economy is critical. SS 304 has roughly double the corrosion resistance and is mandatory for commercial food processing, dairy vats, and long-term wet contact.
Can SS 202 sheets be PVD colour coated or mirror polished? ↓
Yes. SS 202 is widely used as a cost-effective substrate for PVD titanium color coating (Gold, Rose Gold, Black) and 8K mirror polishing for interior architectural wall panels, false ceilings, and dry elevator cabin walls. Explore our Designer SS Sheets Collection.
How can I verify that I received genuine Jindal 202 grade? ↓
Genuine Jindal material comes with factory-printed protective film or inkjet markings displaying heat number and grade. Match this heat number against the EN 10204 Mill Test Certificate supplied with your lot. Mill test certificates are supplied with every lot.
