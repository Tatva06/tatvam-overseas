What Are Stainless Steel 316 / 316L Marine Plates and Where Are They Used?

Tatvam Overseas Inc is a stockholder and exporter of Stainless Steel 316 and 316L marine grade plates, cold rolled sheets, and custom-profile blanks based in Mumbai, India. We maintain robust inventories of mill-certified material designed specifically for corrosive environments, chemical processing tanks, offshore marine vessels, and sterile pharmaceutical fabrication.

Grade 316L (UNS S31603 / 1.4404) is an austenitic chromium-nickel stainless steel modified with 2.0% to 3.0% molybdenum. This deliberate metallurgical addition dramatically elevates resistance to localized pitting and crevice corrosion in chloride-rich environments, seawater splash zones, and organic acid solutions. The low carbon formulation (C ≤ 0.030%) prevents sensitizing grain boundary precipitation during structural welding, guaranteeing high mechanical ductility and corrosion resistance.

Every plate in our inventory conforms strictly to ASTM A240 / ASME SA240 specifications, with dual certification (316/316L) to satisfy the rigorous technical requirements of EPC contractors, refinery engineers, and marine fabricators. Thicknesses range from 0.8 mm light-gauge sheet up to 75 mm heavy hot rolled industrial plate, with widths up to 2500 mm to minimize longitudinal welding seams on large pressure vessel shells.

We provide comprehensive value-added processing including plasma profiling, waterjet cutting, plate beveling, and ultrasonic testing in accordance with ASTM A578 Level II. All consignments are accompanied by EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificates, documenting optical spectrometry, yield stress, impact resistance, and intergranular corrosion test (IGC Practice E) results.

What Factors Determine SS 316L Plate and Sheet Prices in Mumbai?

Stainless steel 316L pricing is subject to key market and manufacturing variables:

  • LME Molybdenum & Nickel Surcharges: The 2-3% molybdenum addition creates a raw material cost premium of 25% to 35% over grade 304.
  • Plate Thickness & Heat Treatment: Heavy-gauge plates (>20 mm) requiring solution quenching and pickling carry specialized processing costs.
  • Custom CNC Profiling: Precision waterjet and plasma cutting to CAD drawings are priced based on linear cutting meters and plate thickness.
  • Order Volume & Tonnage: Project quantities and export containers benefit from tiered volume discounts.
