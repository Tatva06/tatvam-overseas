Material Overview & Specifications

Tatvam Overseas Inc is a premier stockholder and exporter of Stainless Steel 316 and 316L marine grade plates, cold rolled sheets, and custom-profile blanks based in Mumbai, India. We maintain robust inventories of prime mill-certified material designed specifically for corrosive environments, chemical processing tanks, offshore marine vessels, and sterile pharmaceutical fabrication.

Grade 316L (UNS S31603 / 1.4404) is an austenitic chromium-nickel stainless steel modified with 2.0% to 3.0% molybdenum. This deliberate metallurgical addition dramatically elevates resistance to localized pitting and crevice corrosion in chloride-rich environments, seawater splash zones, and organic acid solutions. The low carbon formulation (C ≤ 0.030%) prevents sensitizing grain boundary precipitation during structural welding, guaranteeing high mechanical ductility and corrosion resistance.

Every plate in our inventory conforms strictly to ASTM A240 / ASME SA240 specifications, with dual certification (316/316L) to satisfy the rigorous technical requirements of EPC contractors, refinery engineers, and marine fabricators. Thicknesses range from 0.8 mm light-gauge sheet up to 75 mm heavy hot rolled industrial plate, with widths up to 2500 mm to minimize longitudinal welding seams on large pressure vessel shells.

We provide comprehensive value-added processing including plasma profiling, waterjet cutting, plate beveling, and ultrasonic testing in accordance with ASTM A578 Level II. All consignments are accompanied by 100% genuine Mill Test Certificates to EN 10204 3.1, documenting optical spectrometry, yield stress, impact resistance, and intergranular corrosion test (IGC Practice E) results.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose SS 316L over SS 304 for marine and chemical applications? ↓
SS 316L contains 2-3% molybdenum and higher nickel content, which provides substantially superior resistance to pitting and crevice corrosion caused by salt, chlorides, and harsh industrial acids.
What is the typical lead time for cutting heavy SS 316L plates? ↓
Standard plate profiling and plasma cutting orders are typically completed and prepared for dispatch within 2 to 3 working days from our Mumbai logistics hub.
Can you supply dual-certified 316/316L plates? ↓
Yes, the vast majority of our plate inventory is dual-certified 316/316L, satisfying both the high tensile requirements of Grade 316 and the low-carbon weldability advantages of Grade 316L.
Are export seaworthy packings provided for international orders? ↓
Yes, export consignments are packed on heat-treated, fumigated wooden pallets or heavy-duty wooden crates with waterproof plastic wrapping and metal strap reinforcement.
What documentation accompanies each plate dispatch? ↓
Every dispatch includes the manufacturer's EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificate, PMI verification report, packing list, and commercial invoice.
