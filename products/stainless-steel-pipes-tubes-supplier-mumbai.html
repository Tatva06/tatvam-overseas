What Factors Determine Stainless Steel Pipe and Tube Prices in Mumbai?

Stainless steel pipe and tube pricing depends on manufacturing process and schedule:

  • Seamless vs Welded: Seamless pipes command a premium over ERW/welded pipes due to hot extrusion and cold pilgering processes.
  • Wall Schedule (Sch 40 vs Sch 80/160): Heavy-wall schedules require more raw material per meter, directly increasing weight-based pricing.
  • Grade Selection (304L vs 316L): 316L carries alloy surcharges for molybdenum and higher nickel content.
  • Surface Polish: Mechanical grit polishing (320, 400, 600 grit) or electro-polishing adds specific finishing charges.
