What are SS circles and discs used for?

Circles (also called discs or blanks) are used to make utensils, pots and pans, pressure cookers, kitchenware, tank ends, lids and other drawn or spun parts.

SS 202 or SS 304 circles?

SS 304 forms easily and resists corrosion better, making it the usual choice for food-contact and wet applications. SS 202 is more economical and is widely used for utensils and dry indoor products. Some 200-series variants are tuned for deeper drawing, so tell us your product and process and we will suggest a suitable grade.
