SS 202 vs SS 304 Circles: Grade Selection Guide

SS 202 Circle Blanks

SS 202 replaces a portion of nickel with manganese and nitrogen, delivering a cost-efficient austenitic stainless alloy. SS 202 circles offer dependable formability and high tensile strength for metal spinning and shallow stamping operations.

  • SS 202 is more economical and suits dry indoor use
  • Extensively used for serving plates, trays, and dry storage containers
  • Both grades are widely used for utensils and kitchenware

Explore full grade comparison: SS 202 vs SS 304 Differences or check raw sheets at SS 202 Sheets Mumbai.

SS 304 Circle Blanks

Grade 304 is the classic 18/8 chromium-nickel stainless steel. SS 304 resists corrosion better and suits food contact and wet use. It exhibits exceptional deep-drawing capability, preventing tearing or thinning during intense spinning and stamping presses.

  • Resists acids, water, detergents, and cooking heat
  • Premier choice for pressure cookers, cooking pots, and commercial cookware
  • Both grades are widely used for utensils and kitchenware

SS Circle Weight Calculation Formula & Chart

The theoretical piece weight of a stainless steel circle or disc is calculated by multiplying circular surface area by thickness and material density:

Circle weight (kg) = 0.7854 × D(m)² × T(mm) × density

Standard material densities: 7.93 g/cm³ for Grade 304 and 7.80 g/cm³ for Grade 202. Example benchmark: 300 mm × 1.0 mm yields 0.56 kg in SS 304 and 0.55 kg in SS 202. Check our online metal weight calculator and detailed sheet weight chart guide for more calculations.

Factors Influencing Stainless Steel Circle Prices

Stainless steel circle pricing reflects raw material dynamics and custom cutting parameters. Key factors include:

1. Alloy Grade & Metal Market Rates SS 304 contains substantial nickel and chromium, making it carry a higher cost than manganese-based SS 202. Changing LME nickel and ferrochrome prices directly impact monthly circle rates.
2. Circle Diameter & Sheet Gauge Non-standard diameters require specialized circle-cutting or laser setups. Thicker sheet stock demands heavier press shearing forces, which influences production turnaround and scrap optimization.
3. Cutting Method & Edge Condition Standard rotary sheared circle edges suit straightforward utensil drawing. Deburred or precision circle edges for automated deep-drawing lines involve additional edge smoothing stages.
4. Total Order Volume Larger batch orders optimize coil nesting patterns, minimize stamping scrap, and allow competitive commercial pricing. For broader market pricing dynamics, read our SS Sheet Price per kg Guide.
