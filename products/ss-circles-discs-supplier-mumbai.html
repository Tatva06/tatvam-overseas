SS 202 vs SS 304 Circles: Grade Selection Guide

Choosing between SS 202 and SS 304 circles depends primarily on the end-use environment, corrosion exposure, and target cost per unit. Both grades are widely utilized across Indian cookware and deep-drawing manufacturing.

SS 202 Circles (Jindal J1 / J4)

Economy Grade
  • Best For: Utensils, serving dishes, spice boxes, storage containers, dry kitchenware.
  • Formability: Excellent ductility in J1 grade for multi-stage deep drawing and spinning.
  • Corrosion Resistance: Adequate for indoor, non-saline, and dry environments.
  • Density: 7.80 g/cm³ (lighter weight per blank).

Chemical composition is shown on the mill test certificate supplied with each lot.

SS 304 / 304L Circles (ASTM A240)

Food-Grade Standard
  • Best For: Pressure cookers, milk cans, commercial kitchenware, chemical tanks, tank dished ends.
  • Formability: Superior drawability with high work-hardening resistance.
  • Corrosion Resistance: High resistance to food acids, boiling liquids, and wet cleaning agents.
  • Density: 7.93 g/cm³ (standard austenitic density).

Chemical composition is shown on the mill test certificate supplied with each lot.

SS Circle Weight Calculation Formula & Chart

Calculate theoretical circle weight precisely before ordering to estimate your scrap ratio and material costs per blank:

// Formula:

Circle Weight (kg) = 0.7854 × [Diameter in metres]² × [Thickness in mm] × Density

Where:

• SS 304 Density = 7.93 g/cm³ (0.00793 kg/cm³)

• SS 202 Density = 7.80 g/cm³ (0.00780 kg/cm³)

Factors Influencing Stainless Steel Circle Prices

Stainless steel circle rates in Mumbai are determined by market variables that reflect raw material costs and processing:

Raw Material Index & Grade Surcharges

Global nickel, chromium, and manganese rates directly impact base coil costs. SS 304 carries a premium over SS 202 due to higher nickel content.

Sheet Thickness Gauge

Thin gauges (0.5 mm to 0.8 mm) involve higher rolling and processing passes, whereas thicker gauges (> 3.0 mm) carry lower per-kg rolling premiums.

Cutting Method & Scrap Ratio

Rotary shearing provides the lowest cost per blank for standard sizes. Complex diameters or tight-tolerance cuts may utilize CNC laser cutting.

Dispatch Volume & Packaging

Full-pallet and truckload orders receive bulk wholesale pricing. Export seaworthy wooden box packing is provided on request.

Which stainless steel grade is best for utensil and cookware circles?

SS 304 resists corrosion better and suits food contact and wet cooking applications, including pressure cookers and pots. SS 202 is more economical and suits dry indoor use, serving plates, and storage containers. Both grades are widely used for utensils and kitchenware.

How is the weight of an SS circle calculated?

Circle weight is calculated with the formula: Circle weight (kg) = 0.7854 x [Diameter in metres]^2 x [Thickness in mm] x density (7.93 g/cm3 for SS 304 and 7.80 g/cm3 for SS 202).
