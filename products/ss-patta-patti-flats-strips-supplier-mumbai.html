SS 202 vs SS 304 Patta: Choosing the Right Grade

Understanding the metallurgical and cost differences ensures you select the optimal grade for fabrication, structural integrity, and corrosion durability.

SS 202 Patta & Patti (Jindal J1 / J4)

Economy Grade
  • Best For: Indoor architectural railings, furniture frames, interior partition screens, decorative grills, gate infill patti.
  • Formability: Good bending and punching characteristics.
  • Corrosion Resistance: Engineered for dry indoor environments.
  • Density: 7.80 g/cm³ (economical weight calculation).

Chemical composition is shown on the mill test certificate supplied with each lot.

SS 304 Flat Strips & Bars (ASTM A240)

Industrial Grade
  • Best For: Exterior balconies, coastal structures, food and dairy processing frames, industrial brackets, chemical plant trims.
  • Weldability: Superior weldability without post-weld corrosion susceptibility.
  • Corrosion Resistance: Resistant to weathering, moisture, and industrial atmospheres.
  • Density: 7.93 g/cm³ (standard austenitic density).

SS Flat Bar & Patta Weight Calculation Formula

• SS 304 Density = 7.93 g/cm³

What is the difference between SS patta and SS patti?

In the Indian metal trade, patta and patti both refer to flat strips and flat bars. Patti often refers to narrower cut strips or flat bars used in gates, grills and architectural trims, while patta describes wider slit strips, sheet cuts or continuous strips.

When should I choose SS 202 patta versus SS 304 flat bars?

SS 202 patta is cost-effective for indoor interior decoration, dry indoor furniture frames, and budget gates where humidity is low. SS 304 flat bars are recommended for exterior railings, wet environments, industrial framework, and coastal installations where long-term rust prevention is essential.

How is the weight per metre of SS flat bar calculated?

Weight per linear metre is calculated using: Flat bar weight per metre (kg) = width(mm) x thickness(mm) x density / 1000. For SS 304, use density 7.93; for SS 202, use density 7.80.
