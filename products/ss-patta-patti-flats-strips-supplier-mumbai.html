SS patta and patti: what do the terms mean?

In trade usage, patta and patti both refer to flat stainless steel strips and bars. The terms vary by region and sometimes by width or thickness, so always specify the width, thickness and length you need.

Slit and sheared strips

Strips can be slit from coil or cut from sheet. Custom sizes cut to your requirement. Send grade, size, thickness and quantity.

Uses

Gates and grills, railings, brackets, frames, decorative trims and general fabrication.

SS 202 or SS 304 patta?

SS 202 suits dry indoor use where cost matters. SS 304 is the usual choice for outdoor, wet or food-related use. See our SS 202 vs SS 304 guide.
