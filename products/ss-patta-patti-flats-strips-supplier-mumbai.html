SS 202 vs SS 304 Patta: Choosing the Right Grade

SS 202 Patta & Patti

SS 202 is an austenitic manganese-nitrogen alloyed grade developed to lower raw material cost by reducing nickel content. It provides strong structural yield and tensile strength, making it ideal for indoor architectural fabrications, dry interior partitions, commercial kitchen storage frames, and decorative indoor furniture.

  • Economical price point for competitive architectural bidding
  • Excellent for indoor decorative trim, indoor grills, and panels
  • Not recommended for outdoor coastal railing or chemical exposure

Learn detailed grade differences in our guide: SS 202 vs SS 304 Guide.

SS 304 / 304L Flat Strips

SS 304 is the global benchmark 18/8 chromium-nickel austenitic stainless steel. With high chromium and nickel content, SS 304 flat strips resist atmospheric oxidation, rainwater, industrial weathering, and routine washdown chemicals. It provides superior weldability and longevity for outdoor railings, structural supports, and food-grade machinery frames.

  • Outstanding corrosion resistance for exterior railings and gates
  • Reliable ductile welding without embrittlement
  • Suitable for coastal, architectural and processing plant installations

SS Flat Bar & Patta Weight Calculation Formula

The theoretical weight per linear metre of a stainless steel flat bar, patta, or patti is determined by multiplying its cross-sectional area by the material density:

Flat bar weight per metre (kg) = width(mm) × thickness(mm) × density / 1000

Density factors: 7.93 g/cm³ for Grade 304 / 304L and 7.80 g/cm³ for Grade 202. Use our interactive metal weight calculator or explore our SS sheet and flat weight chart guide for quick estimations.

Pricing Factors for Stainless Steel Patta & Flat Bars

When procuring SS patta or flat bars in the Mumbai metal market, several commercial parameters determine the final quote:

1. Alloy Grade (SS 304 vs SS 202) Grade 304 contains substantial nickel and chromium, making it carry a higher raw material price than manganese-based SS 202. Selecting the right grade for the environment prevents overspending.
2. Strip Width & Thickness Requirements Dimensions cut to specification require tailored machine setup time, which directly influences production turnaround and scrap optimization.
3. Edge Finishing & Deburring Standard sheared or slit edges are suitable for hidden structural fabrication. Deburred or smoothed edges for touch-safe architectural applications involve additional finishing.

What is the difference between SS patta and SS patti?

In the Indian metal trade, patta and patti both refer to flat strips and flat bars. Patti often refers to narrower cut strips or flat bars used in gates, grills and architectural trims, while patta describes wider slit strips, sheet cuts or continuous strips.

When should I choose SS 202 patta versus SS 304 flat bars?

SS 202 patta is cost-effective for indoor interior decoration, dry indoor furniture frames, and budget gates where humidity is low. SS 304 flat bars are recommended for exterior railings, wet environments, industrial framework, and coastal installations where long-term rust prevention is essential.

How is the weight per metre of SS flat bar calculated?

Weight per linear metre is calculated using: Flat bar weight per metre (kg) = width(mm) x thickness(mm) x density / 1000. For SS 304, use density 7.93; for SS 202, use density 7.80.
