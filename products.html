Certified Stainless Steel, High Nickel Alloy & Carbon Steel Stockist, Dealer & Supplier in Mumbai

Tatvam Overseas Inc is a Mumbai-based stockist, dealer and supplier of certified metals, supplying industry since 1992 (formerly Nagmani Metal), with 700+ Tons Ready Stock. Our range covers austenitic, ferritic, duplex and super duplex stainless steels (SS 304/304L, 316/316L, 321, 310S, 904L, 2205), high-temperature boiler alloy steels (ASTM A335 P11, P22, P91), heavy-wall carbon steel seamless pipes (ASTM A106 Gr. B, API 5L), and superalloys including Inconel 600, Inconel 625, Monel 400 and Hastelloy C-276.

Material is supplied with EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificates and heat number traceability, and PMI testing can be arranged on request. Cutting, shearing, slitting and mirror polishing are available on request. We deliver across India through transport partners and ship worldwide with export-grade sea-worthy packaging and complete export documentation.
