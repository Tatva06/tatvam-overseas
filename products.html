Certified Stainless Steel, High Nickel Alloy & Carbon Steel Stockist

Tatvam Overseas Inc maintains an extensive ready inventory of over 700 tons of prime certified metal stock in Mumbai, India. Backed by more than three decades of metallurgical distribution experience since 1992, our material portfolio encompasses austenitic, ferritic, duplex, and super duplex stainless steels (SS 304/304L, SS 316/316L, 321, 310S, 904L, 2205), high-temperature boiler alloy steels (ASTM A335 P11, P22, P91), heavy-wall carbon steel seamless pipes (ASTM A106 Gr. B, API 5L), and superalloys including Inconel 600, Inconel 625, Monel 400, and Hastelloy C-276.

Every order dispatched from our Girgaon central office and Kalbadevi stock hub is 100% traceable to original melt heats, verified via PMI testing, and accompanied by authentic EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificates. We offer precision custom cutting, shearing, slitting, and mirror-polishing services with same-day local dispatch in Mumbai and export-grade sea-worthy packaging for worldwide shipments across 15+ countries.
