Export Documentation for Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia requires a SASO Certificate of Conformity (CoC) for many steel products entering the Kingdom. The CoC is issued by SASO-accredited conformity assessment bodies and must accompany the shipment. Confirm the HS code and product scope with your Saudi customs broker before shipment.

A Chamber of Commerce Certificate of Origin is mandatory. Legalisation by the Indian Embassy / High Commission and apostille may be required for some product categories.

Frequently Asked Questions — Exporting to Saudi Arabia

What products do you export to Saudi Arabia?
SS 304/304L and 316/316L sheets and plates, Duplex 2205, Inconel 625, Hastelloy C276, carbon steel pipes (A106 Gr.B, API 5L), and alloy steel fittings. EPC project packages are welcome.
Is SASO certification required for stainless steel imports?
SASO CoC is required for many steel product categories. The certificate is issued by an accredited body (such as accredited inspection bodies) based on the HS code and product scope. We coordinate with these agencies for pre-shipment testing and CoC issuance.
Can you supply to Jubail Industrial City (JZAN)?
Yes. We supply to trading companies and fabricators based in Jubail, Yanbu, and Dhahran. Delivery is typically CIF King Abdulaziz Port, Dammam, with on-forwarding by the buyer.
Do you offer mill-authorised material for Aramco or SABIC projects?
We supply material with EN 10204 3.1 MTC from mills such as Jindal, SAIL, Outokumpu, and Nippon. Aramco or SABIC approved vendor list status depends on the project specification; contact us with the ITB/MR number for assessment.
What is the payment term for Saudi Arabia orders?
Standard terms are 30% advance with order and 70% against copy of Bill of Lading, or confirmed irrevocable LC at sight. TT in advance for smaller LCL orders.
